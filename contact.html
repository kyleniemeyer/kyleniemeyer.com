Getting in touch

Which address to use depends on what it's about.

University business#

Anything involving Oregon State — prospective students, courses, research collaborations, School of MIME academic programs — should go to kyle.niemeyer@oregonstate.edu.

If you're a current or prospective student, read the lab page and the contact page on my research group site first; they answer most of what people write to ask.

Consulting#

Consulting inquiries go to kyleniemeyer@fastmail.com. See what I do for the kinds of work I take on.

Elsewhere#

  • GitHub — where most of my code lives.
  • Bluesky — I post occasionally and read regularly; DMs and replies reach me.
  • Mastodon — same, less often.
  • LinkedIn — for professional contacts. I usually only accept connections from people I've actually met.

Please don't call. Write first, and we can schedule a call if one would help.
