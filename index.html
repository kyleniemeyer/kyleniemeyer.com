Kyle E. Niemeyer

Engineer and professor. Also a collector of books and an intermittent maker of things.

I'm a Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Oregon State University, where I also serve as Associate School Head for Academic Affairs. My research group uses computational modeling to study fluid flows—combustion, chemical kinetics, numerical methods, and parallel computing—and I spend a good deal of energy on open science and research software.

This is my personal site. Everything about my research, students, publications, and teaching lives on the Niemeyer Research Group site. What's here is the rest: a fuller professional picture, the consulting I do in a personal capacity, and the things I do when I'm not doing any of that.
